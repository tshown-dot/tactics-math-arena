TERM POPPER

Fire terms into the stack. Like terms combine. Make them hit zero to pop them!

1 · COMBINE

Land a term on a like term (same letter, same exponent). They merge into one bubble.

3x + 2x → 5x
2 · CANCEL TO ZERO

If they add up to 0, the whole group pops and everything hanging below falls.

5y − 5y = 0 💥
3 · DON'T MISFIRE

Unlike terms just stick. A misfire burns 3 shots off the ceiling clock.

4x + 4x² ✗   2y + 2 ✗
