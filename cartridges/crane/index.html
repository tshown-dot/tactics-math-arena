📐 enVision 2.0 Key Concept Review:

Grouping Cages & Hierarchy Priority

Operations inside innermost parentheses ( ) must be solved before any outer powers, products, or sums. Equal rank operations (+/− or ×/÷) solve strictly from Left to Right.

🛡️

Site Safety & PEMDAS Demolition Code

Operating a heavy demolition crane requires strict compliance with mathematical structural hierarchy. Striking out of order compromises structural integrity!

1 Grouping Cages ( ) and [ ]

Operations inside inner parentheses ( ) must be cleared first, followed by outer brackets [ ].

2 Exponents & Powers (^)

Powers rank above multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction. Always evaluate powers like 3^2 = 9 before multiplying.

3 Multiplication & Division (×, ÷)

Multiplication and division share equal priority. When multiple exist, evaluate strictly from left to right.

4 Addition & Subtraction (+, −)

Addition and subtraction share equal rank. Always evaluate strictly from left to right.

⚖️ Fraction Girder Dividing Bar

The central fraction dividing bar acts as a major grouping line. You must fully simplify the entire upper girder (numerator) and lower girder (denominator) into single numbers before striking the central division piston!
