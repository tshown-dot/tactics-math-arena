Rules Guide: Order of Operations

1. Grouping symbols first

Do what's inside ( ) or [ ] first. With groups inside groups, start with the innermost one. Finish one group before starting the next.

2 × [10 − (1 + 3)] → 2 × [10 − 4] → 2 × 6 → 12

2. Exponents next

An exponent only belongs to the number (or group) right in front of it.

2 × 5² = 2 × 25 = 50  but  (2 × 5)² = 100

5² means 5 × 5, not 5 × 2.

3. × and ÷, then + and −

× and ÷ are the same rank. + and − are the same rank. Within a rank, go left to right.

40 ÷ 5 × 2 = 8 × 2 = 16 (not 40 ÷ 10)

20 − 5 + 3 = 15 + 3 = 18 (not 20 − 8)

4. Fraction bars group, too

A fraction bar means "divide the whole top by the whole bottom." Simplify the top and bottom completely, then divide.

Two groups side by side? Finish the left one first: in (8 + 4) × (6 − 2), do 8 + 4, then 6 − 2, then multiply.
