Field Guide: Least Common Multiple

1. List and match

4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24

6: 6, 12, 18, 24

The first number in both lists is the LCM: 12. (24 is in both too, but it comes later.)

2. Count by the biggest number

LCM(8, 12)? Count by 12: 12 (÷ 8? no), 24 (÷ 8 = 3, yes!).

The first multiple of the biggest number that the others divide into is the LCM.

3. Quick rules

• If one number divides the other, the bigger one is the LCM: LCM(4, 12) = 12.

• If they share no factor but 1, multiply: LCM(4, 5) = 20.

• If they share a factor, the product is too big: 4 × 6 = 24, but LCM(4, 6) = 12.

• The LCM is never smaller than the biggest number.

4. GCF or LCM?

• GCF: split into the most equal groups, cut the largest equal pieces. Answer is ≤ the numbers.

• LCM: when things happen together again, the fewest to buy so packs match. Answer is ≥ the numbers.
