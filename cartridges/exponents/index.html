Field Guide: Exponent Protocols

1. What is an Exponent?

In bˣ, the base b is the repeated factor, and the exponent x tells how many times to multiply it.

2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 MW

2. The Multiplication Trap!

Don't multiply the base by the exponent! 4³ is NOT 4 × 3 = 12. It's 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.

3. Powers of 10 Rule

10ⁿ always has 1 followed by exactly n zeros. 10⁴ = 10,000 MW (4 zeros).

4. Zero Exponent Rule (b⁰ = 1)

Any non-zero base to the power 0 equals 1. So 7⁰ = 1, not 0 and not 7.

5. Exponent of 1 and Base of 1

b¹ = b (the base used once): 6¹ = 6. And 1 to any power is 1: 1⁵ = 1 × 1 × 1 × 1 × 1 = 1.

6. Swapping Changes the Value

2⁵ = 32 but 5² = 25. Always work out each power before you compare. Sometimes different powers ARE equal: 2⁶ = 4³ = 8² = 64.
