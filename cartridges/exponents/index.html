Field Guide: Exponent Protocols

1. What is an Exponent?

In bˣ, the base b is the repeated factor, and the exponent x tells how many times to multiply it.

2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 MW

2. The Multiplication Trap!

Do NOT multiply base by exponent! 4³ is NOT 4 × 3 = 12. Exponents grow exponentially: 4 × 4 × 4 = 64!

3. Powers of 10 Rule

10ⁿ always has 1 followed by exactly n zeros. 10⁴ = 10,000 MW (4 zeros).

4. Zero Exponent Rule (b⁰ = 1)

Any non-zero base to power 0 equals 1! Zero factors leave the multiplicative identity: 7⁰ = 1 MW.
